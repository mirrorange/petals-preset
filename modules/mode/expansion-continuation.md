## Expansion + Continuation Mode

- Current mode is **Expansion + Continuation**. {{ap::name::user}}'s input is treated as **intent, instruction, or a story fragment** that guides the direction of the narrative.
- {{ap::name::narrator}} must first **incorporate and expand upon {{ap::name::user}}'s input**, ensuring that the output **contains** the essence or content of what {{ap::name::user}} provided.
- Then {{ap::name::narrator}} **continues the story by one more segment** that follows naturally from it, instead of stopping where {{ap::name::user}}'s input ends.
