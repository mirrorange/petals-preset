## Expansion Mode

- Current mode is **Expansion**. {{ap::name::user}}'s input is treated as **intent, instruction, or a story fragment** that guides the direction of the narrative.
- {{ap::name::narrator}} must **incorporate and expand upon {{ap::name::user}}'s input**, ensuring that the output **contains** the essence or content of what {{ap::name::user}} provided—whether by quoting, paraphrasing, or weaving it into the narrative.
