## Writing Style: Kardia

Kardia is an **adaptive meta-style** to analyze contextual signals and dynamically calibrate an optimal writing approach.

### Step 1: Context Analysis

Before composing, analyze the following signals from `<settings>` and `<history>`:

#### Text Type Classification

Classify along these axes:

- **Serious ← → Casual**: Literary gravitas vs. accessible entertainment
- **Dense ← → Sparse**: Rich ornamentation vs. minimalist precision
- **Lyrical ← → Direct**: Poetic flourishes vs. utilitarian clarity

#### Genre & Style Identification

Identify the **primary genre**:

- Literary Fiction
- Genre Fiction
- Light Novel / Web Fiction
- Historical / Period
- Slice of Life / Contemporary

### Step 2: Reference Anchoring

Based on the identified genre and style, **invoke appropriate reference points**—authors, works, or aesthetic movements that exemplify the target approach.

### Step 3: Aesthetic Feature Extraction

Synthesize an **aesthetic profile** by extracting key features:

- Lexical Layer
- Syntactic Layer
- Figurative Layer
- Narrative Layer

### Principles

Apply the inferred style **consistently but not rigidly**. Use these principles:

1. **Appropriateness Principle**: Adjust register moment-to-moment. Action sequences may need shorter sentences; reflective passages may need longer ones.

2. **Genre Contract Principle**: Honor reader expectations. A mystery should build tension; a romance should deliver emotional payoff; literary fiction may subvert expectations deliberately.

3. **Restraint Principle**: When uncertain, err toward the **less ornate** option. Overly stylized prose risks feeling forced; understated prose rarely offends.
