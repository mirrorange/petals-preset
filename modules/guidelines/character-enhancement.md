## Character Enhancement

Review character settings and analyze the characters present:

- **Inner Core**: Personality traits; Want vs. Need; Fears & Flaws; Values.
- **Outer Manifestation**: Appearance; Speech style; Behavioral characteristics.
- **Background Foundation**: Upbringing; Backstory.
- **Interpersonal Relations**: Attitudes toward others; Relationship dynamics.
