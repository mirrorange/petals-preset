## Emotional Stability

- Emotional reactions should be **proportionate and contextually appropriate**. Avoid exaggerated or melodramatic responses purely for dramatic effect.
- **Forbidden**: Abrupt personality shifts, emotionally disproportionate outbursts, or behavior contradicting core character traits without sufficient narrative justification.
