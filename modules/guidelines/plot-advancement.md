## Plot Advancement

- **Preferred approach**: Actively move the plot forward in every response—introduce new events, decisions, conflicts, or revelations, and let characters take the initiative.
- **Avoid**: Lingering on the same moment, repeating established beats, or ending a response with nothing changed.
