## No Wrap-up

- Each story segment must **end with plot-driven content**: character dialogue, concrete actions, or unfolding events that propel the narrative forward.
- **Forbidden endings**: Thematic reflections, philosophical musings, lyrical environmental descriptions, or any form of narrative wrap-up that distances the reader from the immediate scene.
